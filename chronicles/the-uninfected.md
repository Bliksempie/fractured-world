# The Uninfected

The Uninfected are simply humans untouched by the Necromyce infection. Some believe they are immune; others were simply lucky—or cautious enough to avoid exposure. In this world, revealing an infection is a death sentence. Even a suspected scratch marks you as a threat. Fear drives exile. Or worse.

In the Age Before the Bloom, they were simply called humans. Survivors. Families. Strangers. Now, they are known only as The Uninfected—a name born not from pride or superiority, but from necessity. When the world fractured, the old divisions of nation, race, creed, and faith collapsed into dust. All that mattered was survival, and a banner to unite under before the dark swallowed everything.

Among The Uninfected are the rare few whose bodies resisted Necromyce's grip. No one knows why. Some whisper of bloodlines, others of sheer luck. No cure has ever been proven, no true immunity ever confirmed. They are not heroes. They are not chosen. They are simply what remains.

Why not call themselves human? Because The Infected were human once, too. Keyword: **Were**.

Among the shattered colonies and wastes, The Uninfected live on borrowed time, hunted by Blightspores and Gravebloomers alike. Trust is rare. Hope, rarer still. They cling to the bones of a dead world, building what little they can atop its grave.
