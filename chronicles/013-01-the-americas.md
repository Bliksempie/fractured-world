# The Americas

Before the fall, **the Americas** stood as a patchwork of soaring ambition and growing unrest. From gleaming skyscrapers to desolate rural plains, it was a land where prosperity and hardship existed side by side. When Necromyce bloomed, densely packed cities became death traps, and the sprawling open spaces offered little refuge. From the concrete canyons of New York to the sun-scorched streets of São Paulo, hope bled out into the soil almost as fast as the infection spread.

The silver veins of the Andes still glint beneath dust and moss—useless to the world that came after, yet endlessly tempting to tech-hunters who dream of trading old metals for new miracles.

## Natural Disasters

Prone to hurricanes, earthquakes and wildfires.

## Minerals and Resources

Rich in oil, lithium, gold and silver, and agricultural land.

## Survival Insight

Survivors in the mountains may have fared better, relying on the minera riches for tech scavenging or geothermal heat.

## Mountain cunning. Resourceful mindset

Those who begin here benefit from a rugged adaptability—descended from miners, engineers, and survivalists who knew the mountains better than the cities. They start with a higher chance of locating salvageable tech or geothermal warmth, and minor resistance to environmental stress.

## Starting Inventory

🧭 Old Military Compass - Reliable, rugged, slightly scuffed. Allows slightly more accurate directional navigation in foggy/mountain regions. Can be traded for 10 parts or a weathered handgun with no rounds.

## Regional Landmarks and Remnants

### Denver Exclusion Zone

The Hum never left. Survivors near its borders hear whispers, but few cross the chainlink thorns.

### The Gulf Scar

A canyon where oil rigs once floated. It burned for weeks after the Bloom hit — now home to "fire-pocked" Blightspawn.

### Alaska's Broken Line

The northern rail that once ferried cargo through snow now carries something else. Frozen, humming. Waiting.

## Old World Timeline Highlights

### 2020 – The Silence in Seattle

First reports of complete neighborhood blackouts, unresponsive emergency lines, and pets turning feral. Initially blamed on cyberattacks and civil unrest during global pandemic waves.

### 2021 – The Panama Collapse

Key segments of the intercontinental logistics grid fail. Ships pile up at ports, railways grind to a halt, and the Central Grid flickers out. The Americas' supply chain backbone is declared """irreparably compromised."

### 2021 – Operation Wild Throttle (Classified)

Covert American military unit deployed in southern Peru goes dark during """biological containment maneuvers." Only fragmented audio logs remain. The phrase *"it's singing in the rocks"* is repeated.

### 2022 – Treaty of Soot

Last emergency North/South diplomatic summit held in an ash-choked Bogotá. The treaty calls for decentralized governance and emergency civilian bunkering. It is never ratified. Bogotá vanishes three weeks later.

## Historic Cities

Before the Fall, cities across the Americas stood as monuments to ambition—glittering with neon, steel, and ceaseless motion. In the end, their density became their doom. Skyscrapers became spore chimneys. Subways turned to echo chambers for the Hum. Some cities burned quickly; others decayed in silence, their lights fading one by one. Today, these once-mighty urban sprawls are fractured ecosystems—part graveyard, part trap, part myth.

### New York, USA

The city that never slept... sleeps now forever under fungal veils. Once the beating heart of global finance and culture, **New York**'s towering skyline now sags under the weight of decay. Central Park has become a wild thicket, home to both desperate survivors and roving Blightspore packs. The subways—once arteries of human ambition—now echo only with the Hum.

### Los Angeles, USA

**Los Angeles** was a glittering oasis of dreams, where movies and myths were born under neon skies. When Necromyce arrived, the city's sprawling highways became deathtraps, and its opulence offered no shield. Today, the Hollywood sign lies half-swallowed by vines, and the luxury mansions of the hills stand as silent tombs.

### Seattle, USA

**Seattle**'s endless rain once gave it a reputation for melancholy, but it was water that bought it time when the infection spread. For months, communities held out in isolation, fortified among the misty evergreens. Now, the Space Needle juts like a broken finger through grey clouds, and the forests that once protected Seattle harbor worse things than wolves.

### São Paulo, Brazil

One of the largest cities in the southern hemisphere, **São Paulo** thrived on chaos long before the world crumbled. Skyscrapers rose like jagged teeth against the horizon, housing millions. But when Necromyce swept through, density became death. Now, the favelas sprawl endlessly under the shadow of ruined towers, and forgotten fires still flicker at night, far out in the mist.
