## Description

These are the first mutated strain. Blightspores are feral, and were thought to be mindless and chaotic. And yet... something binds them. The Hum. A droning frequency that connects, organizes, and controls them—sometimes with eerie precision.

#### Radiation Zones and the Blisterskins (a.k.a. Bubblers)

When the Necromyce Bloom shattered civilization, it wasn't just the living that were twisted. The old world's scars—broken nuclear plants, devastated reactors, ghost cities of fallout—festered unattended. Over time, certain pockets of lingering radiation became unnatural cradles for new horrors.

Within these zones, a rare mutation sometimes occurred among Blightspores. They did not merely decay or die—they adapted.

These creatures, now called Blisterskins (or Bubblers by those who dare speak of them), are grotesque evolutions of their kin. Their skin crusts over with swollen, pustulant growths—living tumors that pulse with toxic decay. They hum not in the voice of the Hum, but in a broken, sluggish echo, a sound that does not tie them to their own kind.
<!-- /show-if -->

Blisterskins or Bubblers cannot leave their radiation nests.

Legends say that the poisoned blood binding them unravels the moment they step too far from the irradiated earth. Few who have seen a Bubbler cross a dead zone's edge have lived to tell of the way they collapse into shrieking heaps of ash and bile.

Yet their presence strengthens the contamination. A Bubbler's death too near its home can cause the radiation to spread, corrupting new ground, growing the dead zone wider, stronger. Only through cleverness—luring them far and killing them cleanly—can a survivor hope to reclaim these cursed spaces.

Still, rumors persist that hidden wonders remain buried in these dead lands. Secrets. Salvage. Salvation.

Those brave or desperate enough to breach a radiation zone must balance their greed against the terrible cost of failure. In Fractured World, every scar on the earth still bleeds. Some wounds were never meant to heal.

### Titans

Few Blightspores survive long enough to reach this terrible final state. **Titans** are mountains of rot and muscle, layers of hardened spore-matter encasing what little remains of the host. Slow but unstoppable, they smash through barricades, walls, and defenses alike. They don't run. They don't need to. A Titan's footfall can crack pavement—and your bones if you're foolish enough to stand in its path.

### Bloomspawn

Where Titans are mountains of corrupted muscle, Bloomspawn are something worse: intent given form. A Bloomspawn is not a single infected body—it is a fused abomination, a grotesque colony of Blightspore hosts knotted together by fungal growth, nerve, and blind instinct. No two Bloomspawn are exactly alike. Some crawl. Some slither. Some walk on fused limbs like broken gods. All of them spread Necromyce spores in their wake, leaving landscapes where nothing human survives. To face a Bloomspawn is not to fight. It is to choose between escape... or oblivion.

### The Black Choir

Among survivors and even whispering Gravebloomer elders, there is talk of **The Black Choir**—a supposed Blightspore phenomenon where entire hordes move in eerie, perfect unison, not merely through instinct, but through something deeper. A pure, brutal harmony of mindless will.

An unnatural silence often follows these events, leaving villages abandoned without a trace of struggle—as if The Black Choir simply swept them away.

No one agrees whether it is a true evolution—or simply the last, horrific illusion before death.

Some scholars (and a few mad Gravebloomer exiles) claim that if enough Blightspores gather together, and under the right circumstances, something inside their infected blood "awakens," briefly linking them into a temporary hive mind far more dangerous than their normal animalistic state.

### The Blight King

Among hushed voices around dying campfires, there persists a terrifying legend—one so blasphemous that even most Gravebloomers refuse to acknowledge it:

> *“There was once a Blight King — a Blightspore who did not lose his mind.”*
<!-- /show-if -->

Unlike the shrieking hordes bound by instinct and Hum, the Blight King was said to retain a brutal form of intelligence. Not human kindness. Not reason. Pure malevolent cunning fused with the raw, infectious hunger of the Blight.

He rose during the early days, when Necromyce's children were still chaotic, when colonies clashed and devoured one another without thought. The Blight King united **the Hum** of dozens of disparate colonies into a single terrible symphony, orchestrating mass assaults on survivor settlements. Entire regions were swallowed into silence overnight. Gravebloomer camps were razed, their memories burned to ash.

They say he built a kingdom in the ruins—a place where Blightspores marched in disciplined phalanxes, where the Hum sang strategies, not just madness.

But how did it end? Nobody knows.

**Stories are few and varying:**

- Some claim his kingdom consumed itself, the sheer mass of Hum creating a psychic collapse that shredded every mind at once.
- Others believe the Gravebloomers rallied in secret and unleashed a blood ritual so devastating that even the Blight King could not endure it.
- A few whisper he was never truly defeated, only slumbering somewhere deep beneath the earth, his corrupted mind waiting to rise again if the Blight grows strong enough...

**Other rumors that have been heard are:**

- **The Throne of Spines:** Said to still exist somewhere in the deep ruins — a towering fungal growth shaped like a twisted throne, humming so low it rattles teeth.
- **The Discord:** Some surviving Blight colonies show strange "anomalies" in their Hum, as if echoes of the Blight King's unified song still linger in their blood.
- **Dreams of the Crown:** Very rarely, Gravebloomer Bishops are driven mad by sudden dreams of a fungal crown calling to them.

Others whisper he was the last man who dared rule over the infected, a tyrant who wore the blight like armor and waged war across the poisoned zones. No one agrees on the truth.

According to wandering scholars, there was once a figure known as **The Last King of Blight** who emerged from the fallout, neither fully Gravebloomer nor Blightspore—something different, something terrible.

It's said he learned to move freely between radiation zones, using the poisoned earth itself to heal and command legions of the deformed. Survivors tell tales of banners stitched from the skins of both Blightspores and men, flying above shattered strongholds before they were swallowed again by death and silence.

Whether The Last King of Blight still roams, or whether he ever lived at all, none can say. But in the darkest corners of abandoned colonies, his legend stirs the embers of both fear and hope:

> *That even monsters might be kings… and kings, monsters.*
