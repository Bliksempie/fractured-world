# The Necromyce Infection

## Type

Cataclysmic Event

## Description

The **Necromyce infection** marked the end of the Old World. An unstoppable fungal plague that rose from the earth, mutating all organic life it touched.

## First Recorded

**Day Zero**, exact origin point unknown.

## Known Facts

- Spread through soil, water, and air.
- Infected vegetation and animals act as silent carriers.
- Led to the collapse of all known global civilization within 8 months.

## Unconfirmed Myths

- Some believe Necromyce was artificially created.
- Others whisper that it was "awakened" from the deep earth.
